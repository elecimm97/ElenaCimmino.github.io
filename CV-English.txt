PROFILE
Medical biotechnologist with extensive experience in bioinformatics and computational biology, and a PhD in Molecular Medicine and Medical Biotechnology. My work spans computational tools and pipelines for biological, genomic and imaging data, quantitative modelling of cellular systems, and, more recently, spatial and single-cell transcriptomics to study the tumour microenvironment and cellular communication and signalling.

RESEARCH
During my PhD and subsequent research at CEINGE, I developed quantitative methods and software to study cultured cells and genomic data. This work included models of cell shape and morphology and tools for simulating cell populations, integrated into SimulCell. In parallel, I developed scientific web applications and gained experience in manipulating, visualising and comparing genomic sequences and assemblies, contributing to ChromoMapper and ChromoMapperWeb. My recent research focuses on spatial and single-cell transcriptomics to study cellular interactions and signalling programmes in the tumour microenvironment. I also develop reproducible, modular Python/R pipelines for spatial transcriptomics analysis.
